# Contact MarkdownCanDo

The most reliable way to report a problem or suggest an improvement is through the project's public [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/gantrol/markdown-can-do/issues).

## What to include

For a bug or broken example, please include:

- the page URL;
- what you expected to happen;
- what happened instead;
- your browser and device when relevant;
- a small Markdown example that reproduces the problem.

For a content correction, link to the affected heading and provide a reliable source when the change concerns a factual claim.

## Privacy requests

GitHub issues are public. Do not post passwords, private documents, personal identifiers, or other sensitive information. For general information about site data practices, read the [privacy policy](/privacy).

You can also learn more [about MarkdownCanDo](/about) or review the [terms of use](/terms).
